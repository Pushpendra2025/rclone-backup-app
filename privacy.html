Privacy policy

rclone backup (personal) · last updated 2026-10-11

Who this covers

This app is used by one person, its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

Only the files it creates in the owner's Google Drive. It requests the drive.file permission, so it can't see, read or change any other file in the Drive. It doesn't access email, contacts, calendars, photos or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Only to store and retrieve the owner's encrypted backups. The backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before they are uploaded.

Sharing, selling and tracking

None. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third parties. The data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computer.

Storage and deletion

Backups are kept under a retention schedule (recent daily, weekly and monthly versions) and older ones are deleted automatically. The owner can delete everything at any time from Google Drive, and can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
